# Transformative Leftover Cascade Planner (Wave 19 #536)

## 1. Problem
Traditional leftovers in meal planning suffer from a behavioral bounce rate: users do not want to see "eat leftover roast chicken again" multiple nights in a row.

## 2. Solution: Intentional Component Remix Graph
Rather than reheating identical meals, producer dishes intentionally yield cooked components that become distinct downstream dish identities:
- **Sunday Herb Roast Chicken** → **Tuesday Chicken Tinga Tacos**, **Wednesday Chicken Fried Rice**, or **French Tarragon Chicken Salad**.
- **Slow Cooker Pulled Pork** → **Pork Enchiladas Verde** or **Caramelized Onion Quesadillas**.
- **Roasted Veggie Medley** → **Herb & Veggie Frittata**.

## 3. Grocery & Safety Efficiency
- **Fresh Finishers Only**: Downstream recipes on the grocery list request only the garnishes and wraps (e.g. tortillas, cilantro, limes, tarragon) without re-purchasing proteins.
- **Lineage Clarity**: Cards explicitly label their provenance ("Remix from Sunday Roast Chicken") while presenting a full-fledged unique dish.
